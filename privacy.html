Privacy & Data Practices

Privacy Policy

How The Badger Co. handles website information and BadgerFit beta application data. Last updated: 1 October 2026.

Who operates BadgerFit

BadgerFit is operated by The Badger Co., Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, India. For questions, data access, correction requests, or grievances, contact us directly at support@thebadgerco.in.

Website signups & inquiries

The public website waitlist and contact forms collect your email address (and optional name or message) via FormSubmit directly to support@thebadgerco.in. This data is used solely to provide product updates and access announcements. To update your preferences or request deletion from our mailing list, email support@thebadgerco.in.

Website analytics vs. app isolation

Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics are used exclusively on our public marketing website (thebadgerco.in) to evaluate aggregate site traffic and page engagement. Google Analytics is not embedded or used inside the BadgerFit application. We never send your email address, account identifier, Fit Check answers, Foot Check answers, diabetes responses, measurements, scan records, or photos to Google Analytics.

BadgerFit application data collection

Within the BadgerFit application, data collection is limited to what is necessary to operate shoe-fit and comfort guidance features:

  • Account credentials: Sign-in email address and authentication tokens via Supabase Auth or Google OAuth.
  • 18+ eligibility: Confirmation that you are aged 18 or older, required during registration.
  • Saved footwear (My Shoes): Footwear brand, model, size, shoe type, and fit notes you choose to save.
  • Fit Check assessments: Structured answers regarding shoe-specific discomfort and saved recommendation snapshots.
  • Foot Check assessments: Structured answers regarding everyday foot sensations, standing hours, footwear types, and an optional response regarding diabetes history.
  • Beta feedback: Voluntary feedback and bug reports submitted through in-app forms or email.
  • Legacy scan records: Historical numeric foot measurements from earlier testing (if linked to your historical test account). No photos are stored.
  • Payments: We do not collect payment details or process transactions in the beta.

Camera & optical scanning status

Foot Scan (optical camera measurement) is paused during the beta (SCAN_AVAILABLE = false). The live BadgerFit beta does not capture, process, or store biometric foot photos, camera video streams, or optical scan models. All Fit Check and Foot Check recommendations are generated exclusively from structured self-reported questionnaires.

Non-medical boundary

BadgerFit is an educational shoe-fit and comfort tool, not a medical device. It does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment plans, or clinical foot evaluations. Content, tips, and guided movement suggestions are educational footwear comfort aids. Users with persistent pain, injury, numbness, swelling, skin changes, or diabetes-related concerns should consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Research protocol boundary

Account creation and use of Fit Check, Foot Check, or feedback features do not constitute clinical or academic research participation. No separate clinical research protocol is active in this beta.

Third-party service providers

We use trusted infrastructure providers strictly to deliver our services:

  • Supabase: User authentication and application database storage.
  • Google Identity: Optional Google OAuth sign-in.
  • Hostinger: Transactional email delivery (such as password reset notices).
  • Vercel & Render: Web application and backend API hosting.
  • FormSubmit: Website waitlist and contact form processing.

These providers process data only as needed to deliver their services to us and are bound by confidentiality obligations. We do not sell or rent personal data to third parties or advertisers.

Device & local browser storage

The BadgerFit application uses browser localStorage strictly for functional necessities: maintaining your sign-in session (sb-*-auth-token), saving in-progress assessment drafts, and storing theme preferences. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, session storage trackers, or browser databases inside the app.

Account deletion & data management

You have full control over your personal data.

In-app self-service deletion: You can permanently delete your BadgerFit account directly inside the app at any time by navigating to Profile → Manage Data → Delete account, reading the confirmation prompt, and typing DELETE. This immediately and permanently deletes your Supabase Auth account, user profile, 18+ eligibility status, saved shoes, Fit Check cases, Foot Check responses, beta feedback, and legacy scan records from our active databases.

Web & email deletion request: If you cannot access the application, you can submit a deletion request via our dedicated Account Deletion page or email support@thebadgerco.in from your registered account email. Deletion is executed upon verification of account ownership.

Data retention

We retain account-associated data while your account is active or until you delete specific records or your account. When your account is deleted, all user records are permanently purged from active databases. Standard, non-user-identifiable server access and infrastructure logs expire automatically within 30 days for security and rate limiting.

Eligibility (18+)

BadgerFit is intended exclusively for individuals aged 18 and older. Explicit confirmation that you are 18 or older is required during registration. Do not create or use a BadgerFit account if you are under 18.

Security

Account-backed records are stored with Supabase and transmitted using encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and encrypted at rest. Access is limited to operating and supporting BadgerFit.

Contact & grievances

For privacy inquiries, data access or deletion requests, or grievances, please contact: support@thebadgerco.in.
